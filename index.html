    Getting around and paying

    • Muni is about $2.75 a ride when you tap a contactless card or Clipper, or $3 in cash. Confirm the current fare on sfmta.com.
    • A cable car ride is $8 one way. A 1-day Visitor Passport is about $14 and covers cable cars, so it pays off after roughly five rides.
    • Muni stopped selling single-ride tickets in its app on Sep 1. Tap a card or use Clipper.
    • Alcatraz tickets sell out weeks ahead. Buy only from the official Alcatraz City Cruises site, because resellers add fees.
    • Asian Art Museum is free on the first Sunday of each month, and the next one is Nov 1.

    Costs are estimates. Club covers and anything marked "check" are not included in totals. Hours shown are for the day you pick, and the route planner uses them. Confirm hours before you go.
